Pico wallet: ATECC608 wires (the white/yellow/black/red bag)

The bag of white / yellow / black / red JST cables into the LCD board header, beside the Pico pins. USB at the top. Count down from the USB end.

Right side, top down: VBUS, VSYS, GND (white), 3V3_EN, 3V3 (yellow). Skip 1st, 2nd and 4th.

Left side, top down: GP0, GP1, GND, GP2, GP3, GP4 (black), GP5 (red).

Push the bare ends into the LCD board's socket beside the Pico pins. Tug lightly. Jam the breakout flat in the gap.

Bare pins on the Pico itself are in the same order, board face up, USB at top.

Rule: the plug is GND, V+, SDA, SCL from the GND end. This bag colors that white, yellow, black, red. Trust the order, not the colors.
